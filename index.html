How I Approach a Support Issue

1. Understand

Listen to the user, clarify symptoms, identify scope and impact, and record useful details.

2. Investigate

Check likely causes systematically, use appropriate tools, and avoid making assumptions.

3. Resolve or escalate

Follow approved procedures, protect user data, and escalate when access or expertise is needed.

4. Verify and document

Confirm the user can work again, record the outcome, and note any follow-up or prevention steps.
