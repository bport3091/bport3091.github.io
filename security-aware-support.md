# Security-Aware Help Desk Checklist

**Type:** Learning checklist.

## When a user reports a suspicious email
- Ask whether they clicked a link, opened an attachment, or entered credentials.
- Follow the organization's official incident-reporting process.
- Escalate quickly to the security team if credentials may be exposed or malware may have run.
- Preserve the message and relevant details according to policy.
- Do not shame the user or promise that an incident is harmless.

## Protecting information during support
- Verify identity before changing accounts or sharing sensitive information.
- Use approved remote-support tools only.
- Lock the screen when stepping away.
- Avoid putting passwords, personal data, or confidential company information in tickets.
- Follow least-privilege and data-handling rules.
- Keep operating systems and applications updated through approved processes.

## Reflection prompts
- Which risks can a help desk technician identify early?
- What information belongs in a ticket, and what should be excluded?
- When should a technician escalate rather than continue investigating?
