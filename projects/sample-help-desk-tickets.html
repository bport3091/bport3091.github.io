PROJECT 03 | SUPPORT SIMULATION

Help Desk Ticket Samples

Examples of how IT support technicians document technical issues, troubleshooting steps, and resolutions.

About This Project

These are simulated help desk tickets created for learning and portfolio purposes. They are not real customer incidents.

The goal is to practice clear documentation, problem-solving, prioritization, and communication.

Ticket HD-001: Wi-Fi Connection Problem

Reported issue: A user cannot connect to the office Wi-Fi.

Troubleshooting: Check whether Wi-Fi is enabled, confirm the correct network, review connection status, and inspect IP configuration.

Example resolution: In this practice scenario, Wi-Fi was disabled. Enabling it restored the connection.

Verification: Confirm the user can access approved work resources.

Status: Resolved in the simulated scenario.

Ticket HD-002: User Cannot Sign In

Reported issue: An employee cannot sign in to their account.

Troubleshooting: Record the error message, verify the account name and keyboard layout, and check for known service issues.

Next step: Follow the approved identity-verification and password-reset procedures or escalate to the account administrator.

Security note: Never ask a user to share their password.

Ticket HD-003: Printer Not Responding

Reported issue: A user's print job does not print.

Troubleshooting: Check the selected printer, power, error indicators, and print queue. Determine whether other users are affected.

Next step: Follow approved printer troubleshooting procedures or escalate to the appropriate support team.

Ticket HD-004: Slow Computer

Reported issue: A computer is running slower than usual.

Troubleshooting: Ask when the issue began, review Task Manager, check available storage, and determine whether one application or the entire computer is affected.

Next step: Make appropriate changes, if authorized, and verify whether performance improves.

Ticket HD-005: Suspicious Email

Reported issue: An employee receives a suspicious email asking for credentials or urgent payment.

Troubleshooting: Ask whether the employee clicked a link, opened an attachment, or entered account information.

Next step: Follow the organization's incident-reporting process and promptly notify the designated security team.

Security note: Do not delete evidence or investigate beyond your authorization.

Standard Ticket Documentation

  • Ticket number and date
  • Reported issue
  • Business impact and urgency
  • Troubleshooting steps performed
  • Findings and resolution
  • Verification of the outcome
  • Escalation or follow-up actions
  • Final status
