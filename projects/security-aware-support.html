Security-Aware Support

IT Help Desk Portfolio | Brian Portilla

Demonstrating how help desk professionals can support users while protecting company systems, accounts, and information.

Project Overview

Help desk employees play an important role in cybersecurity. They help users solve technical problems while making sure that sensitive information and company systems stay protected.

This project explains basic security practices that an entry-level IT support technician should follow when assisting users.

1. Password and Account Security

  • Encourage users to create strong, unique passwords.
  • Recommend multifactor authentication when available.
  • Never ask users to share their passwords.
  • Follow company procedures for password resets and account recovery.
  • Never bypass identity verification to save time.

2. Phishing and Suspicious Messages

Phishing messages may try to trick employees into sharing passwords, opening harmful attachments, or visiting fake websites.

Warning signs to look for:
  • Unexpected requests for passwords or personal information.
  • Links that lead to unfamiliar or suspicious websites.
  • Unexpected attachments.
  • Messages that create urgency or threaten account closure.
  • Requests to send money or change payment information.

If a user reports a suspicious message, I would follow the organization's reporting process and avoid opening links or attachments in the message.

3. Protecting Sensitive Information

  • Access only the information needed to complete an assigned task.
  • Verify a user's identity before making account changes.
  • Do not share confidential information with unauthorized people.
  • Lock workstations when leaving them unattended.
  • Follow company policies for storing, sending, and disposing of data.

4. Responding to a Possible Security Incident

  1. Listen carefully to the user and gather basic details.
  2. Record what happened, including relevant error messages and times.
  3. Follow company procedures for reporting the incident.
  4. Escalate the issue to the appropriate security team when necessary.
  5. Document the actions taken and follow approved instructions.
Important: A help desk technician should not investigate beyond their authorization, delete evidence, or promise that a system is safe before the appropriate team has assessed the situation.

5. Example Help Desk Scenario

Situation: An employee contacts the help desk after entering their password on a website they now believe was fake.

Support response:

  • Remain calm and gather the details needed to understand the report.
  • Follow company procedures to report and escalate the incident.
  • Help the employee follow approved account-protection steps.
  • Recommend changing the compromised password through the official process.
  • Document the incident without recording the user's password.

Goal: Help protect the employee's account and reduce the chance of further damage.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Security awareness
  • Account and password safety
  • Phishing awareness
  • Incident reporting and escalation
  • Technical documentation
  • Following security policies and procedures

Project Reflection

This project shows why security must be part of everyday IT support. A help desk technician needs to solve technical problems while protecting users and company information. Following established procedures, documenting issues, and knowing when to escalate a problem are important skills for an entry-level IT support role.
